B

Flow-through bridge

Illustrative · AED '000

Hotel revenue rose AED 300k on last year, yet GOP rose by just AED 66k. That is 22% flow-through against a 50% target in the budget. The bridge shows where the missing AED 84k went, and each red bar becomes a question for the operator.

Flow-through = change in GOP ÷ change in revenue = 66 ÷ 300 = 22%

Break-even saving is the smallest yearly saving that still returns the owner's rate. The gap between that and the claimed saving is the margin for error the owner is buying.

F

Pre-opening

Real method · illustrative checklist

Before Fresh Bake's first store opened, I worked on its launch sales scenarios at 25%, 50% and 75% of capacity, each with a food and drink split and weekly product usage. A pre-opening budget for a hotel outlet deserves the same three cases, because the opening month rarely lands on the middle one.

  • Pre-opening payroll ramp set against the confirmed opening date, with the cost of a two-week slip shown
  • Training weeks per role, costed and in the budget
  • Opening stock sized to the low case, with reorder points for the high case
  • Snag list closed and signed before handover from the contractor
  • Brand-standard and system costs from the new flag included from day one

H

Questions for the operator

  1. Split the revenue variance into volume and rate for rooms, and covers and spend for F&B.
  2. Show payroll against a flexed budget as well as the original one.
  3. Explain any line more than 5% adverse in writing, with an owner and a date.
  4. Put the next three months' forecast next to the budget.
  5. List open capex requests with payback and status.
  6. Report review themes, with the score alongside.
  7. Show utilities per occupied room against last year.
  8. Confirm brand fees and system charges match the management agreement.
